About My Approach

A Grounded, Calming Environment For Your Growth

I believe therapy should feel accessible, genuine, and unhurried. Too often, people feel hesitant to reach out because of lengthy intake paperwork or cold clinical barriers. My practice is centered around nature-inspired, calming conversations where you can express yourself freely.

During our initial 15-minute consultation, my priority is simply getting to know you, answering any questions you have about my therapeutic style, and helping you decide with total confidence if I am the right guide for your journey.
