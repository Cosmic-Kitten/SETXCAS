Philosophy & Approach

Finding the right therapist shouldn't feel exhausting.

I believe therapy starts with comfort and genuine human connection. My practice is designed around a calm, nature-inspired environment where you can slow down and feel heard without pressure.

These initial 15-minute consultations are entirely informal. There are no clinical evaluations or tedious intake questionnaires. It is simply an opportunity for us to talk, for me to explain my therapeutic process, and for you to gauge how comfortable you feel working with me.

Why a 15-Minute Chat?

  • 1

    Mutual Fit: Therapy is deeply personal. You deserve to feel confident in your therapist.

  • 2

    Zero Friction: Skip the forms. Just pick a time that works and show up.
