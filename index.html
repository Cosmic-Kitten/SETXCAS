Philosophy & Approach

Finding the right therapist shouldn't feel exhausting.

I believe therapy starts with comfort and genuine human connection. My practice is designed around a calm, nature-inspired environment where you can slow down and feel heard without pressure.

These initial 15-minute consultations are entirely informal. There are no clinical evaluations or tedious intake questionnaires. It is simply an opportunity for us to talk, for me to explain my therapeutic process, and for you to gauge how comfortable you feel working with me.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to fill out paperwork before our first meeting?

No. For these short 15-minute introductory meetings, there is zero paperwork or forms required. The goal is simply to chat, see if we are a good fit, and discuss my therapy process.

How do these 15-minute consultations take place?

When you book, you receive a Google Calendar invite with a secure meeting link or phone contact details, making it seamless to connect.

What happens after the consultation?

If we both feel that working together is a good fit, we can schedule your first full session and go over any standard onboarding details at that time.
